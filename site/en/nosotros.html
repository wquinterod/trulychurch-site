About us

A family that walks together

We believe everyone can know God, grow in faith and find their place to serve.

Our story

From a living room to the whole city

  • 2011 · A table and twelve people

    Three families start meeting on Thursdays in a Clear Lake home to pray and share dinner.

  • 2014 · Our first Sunday

    We rented a community hall and held our first service with eighty people.

  • 2018 · Groups in every neighborhood

    Connection groups multiply: today there are twelve, in homes across the city.

  • 2023 · A home of our own

    We moved to 100 Demo Way and added a second Sunday service.

  • Today · Belong, grow and serve

    Same conviction as day one: faith is better lived in community.
